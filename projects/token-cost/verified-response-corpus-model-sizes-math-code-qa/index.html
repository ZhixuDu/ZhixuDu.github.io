Scoring LLM Size Ladders Against Verified Math, Code, QA Answers

Is there a study scoring many model sizes against verified responses on math, code, and QA benchmarks?

Answer

Du et al. (2026) build checker-verified references from Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct and Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct on twelve benchmarks totalling 50,019 prompts, and score them token by token under teacher forcing with several model ladders: Qwen2.5 from 0.5B to 72B (seven sizes), OLMo-2 (1B, 7B, 13B, 32B), DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen (1.5B, 7B, 14B, 32B) and Falcon3 (1B, 3B, 7B, 10B).

The benchmarks and checkers are GSM8K (1,319 prompts, numeric answer with tolerance), MATH-500 (500, symbolic equivalence via Math-Verify), HumanEval (164, official tests), a LiveCodeBench subset (219 problems with stdin/stdout tests), MBPP+ (378), AIME 2024 (30), ARC-Challenge (1,172), BBH (6,511), MMLU-Pro (12,032), MMLU (14,042), HellaSwag (10,042) and NQ-Open (3,610, normalised exact alias match). GSM-Symbolic adds 5,000 prompts as a separate robustness set. On GSM8K, for example, 1,281 Qwen and 1,288 Llama responses were verified, with 1,275 in the intersection. References are sampled four times per prompt at temperature 0.7 and top-p 0.95, keeping the earliest passing sample, and a Qwen3-30B-A3B mixture-of-experts generator supplies extra reference text.

The reasoning-distilled ladder behaves much like its base family. On the core Qwen-written references, R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5B reproduces 89.80-92.27% of tokens, compared with 91.86-95.24% for Qwen2.5-0.5B, and its most expensive 10% of tokens hold 67.19-74.76% of estimated FLOPs. Its unresolved rates are higher, 3.59-5.73% versus at most 0.76% for the Qwen panel. Its difficulty ordering correlates 0.255/0.379 with Qwen and 0.267/0.369 with OLMo on Qwen/Llama text, below the 0.322/0.417 between Qwen and OLMo.

Cross-family agreement on hard positions is positive on every benchmark, from 0.286 (MBPP+) to 0.440 (HumanEval) on Qwen text.

Setup

Measurement panels of fifteen models: Qwen2.5 (0.5B to 72B, seven sizes), OLMo-2 (1B, 7B, 13B, 32B) and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen (1.5B, 7B, 14B, 32B), with Falcon3 (1B, 3B, 7B, 10B) for cross-family replication. References are checker-verified responses from Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct (four samples per prompt at temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95, earliest passing sample kept), with Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct as a second generator. Core datasets are GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval (1,800 Qwen-written references: 1,281, 375 and 144), within twelve benchmarks totaling 50,019 prompts. Teacher-forced scoring runs in vLLM 0.14.1 with BF16 on eight 80 GB A100-SXM4 GPUs; uncertainty uses 2,000 item-bootstrap resamples.

References are checker-passing responses, so coverage of unsuccessful trajectories and the hardest problems is limited (for example 6 of 30 AIME items verified for Qwen).

Source

What Does a Token Cost? A Mixture-of-Agents Measurement of Sufficient Per-Token Compute. Zhixu Du, Weijia Han, Hai (Helen) Li, Yiran Chen. Under review, 2026. Evidence: Section 4, Appendix A, Tables A1, A2.

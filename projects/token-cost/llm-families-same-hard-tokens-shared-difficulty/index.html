Do Different LLM Families Find the Same Tokens Hard?

Do different LLM families find the same tokens hard to predict?

Answer

To a measurable degree, yes. Du et al. (2026) find that the Qwen2.5, OLMo-2 and Falcon3 families agree on which word positions need larger models. After controlling for generator surprisal, probe surprisal and position, pairwise partial Spearman correlations pooled over GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval range from 0.322 to 0.398 on Qwen-written references and from 0.417 to 0.475 on Llama-written references.

Each token gets a reproduction index, the smallest model in a family's size ladder that reproduces it under teacher forcing. Because families use different tokenizers, predictions are aligned to common words, each word takes the largest index among its tokens, and unresolved words get an index above the panel. The partial correlation removes the ranks of generator surprisal, probe surprisal (from Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) and log position before correlating. Core Qwen text contributes 319,336 words from 1,800 items and Llama text 302,959 words from 1,797 items, with 2,000 item-bootstrap resamples.

Pairwise, Qwen2.5/OLMo-2 gives 0.322 and 0.417 on Qwen and Llama text, Qwen2.5/Falcon3 0.380 and 0.475, and OLMo-2/Falcon3 0.398 and 0.447. The reasoning-distilled R1-Distill-Qwen panel correlates 0.255/0.379 with Qwen and 0.267/0.369 with OLMo. The association is positive on all twelve benchmarks, changes by less than 0.05 under additional controls and alternative criteria, reaches 0.520 on text from a Qwen3-30B-A3B mixture-of-experts generator, and is stronger than greedy or soft speculative-rejection signals on the same text.

The paper limits the claim. The comparisons do not separately control architecture, tokenization, training data or instruction tuning, R1-Distill-Qwen shares Qwen lineage, and independent families may share training data. The results establish a shared ordering within the evaluated panels, without identifying its source or showing that a map transfers between families.

Setup

Measurement panels of fifteen models: Qwen2.5 (0.5B to 72B, seven sizes), OLMo-2 (1B, 7B, 13B, 32B) and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen (1.5B, 7B, 14B, 32B), with Falcon3 (1B, 3B, 7B, 10B) for cross-family replication. References are checker-verified responses from Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct (four samples per prompt at temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95, earliest passing sample kept), with Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct as a second generator. Core datasets are GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval (1,800 Qwen-written references: 1,281, 375 and 144), within twelve benchmarks totaling 50,019 prompts. Teacher-forced scoring runs in vLLM 0.14.1 with BF16 on eight 80 GB A100-SXM4 GPUs; uncertainty uses 2,000 item-bootstrap resamples.

Pooled over GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval for the Qwen2.5, OLMo-2 and Falcon3 panels on Qwen- and Llama-written verified references.

Accuracy Loss When a Small LLM Finishes a Large LLM's Solution

How much accuracy does a small model lose when it finishes a reasoning chain started by a large model?

Answer

It depends strongly on the small model's capacity and on how late the handover happens. Du et al. (2026) let Qwen2.5 models from 0.5B to 32B complete a verified Qwen2.5-72B prefix starting at character fractions 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 0.9 of the reference. Pooled across GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval, the 0.5B model loses 54.08 accuracy points relative to a 72B continuation when generating from the prompt and 37.64 points when starting from the midpoint. The 32B model stays near zero.

Per dataset (Table A9), on GSM8K the 0.5B model's loss shrinks from 52.88 points at the prompt to 35.08 at the midpoint and 10.62 at 0.9, while 7B loses 4.51 at the prompt and 0.72 at 0.9. On MATH-500, 0.5B loses 55.76, 45.59 and 16.35 at those points, and 32B ranges from +1.17 at the prompt to +0.02 at 0.9. On HumanEval, 0.5B loses 60.98 from the prompt and 18.56 at 0.9, and 32B loses 2.80 from the prompt.

Both orderings are regular. Across 35,156 paired item, size and start cells, the pooled size ordering has zero inversions among 15 adjacent comparisons, and the start-position ordering has two inversions among 16. Handing over later reduces the cost of switching to a smaller model, and larger models reduce it further.

Bounded delegation is far cheaper than full completion: in a separate sweep, a 7B model loses 1.50 points after 64 delegated tokens before 72B resumes. The paper relates these experiments to Reasoning Relay, LLM Shepherding and RelayGen, and frames them as characterising the gap between conditional reproduction and sustained continuation rather than introducing model switching.

Setup

The 1,758 core problems with verified references from both generators (1,275 GSM8K, 347 MATH-500, 136 HumanEval). A smaller Qwen2.5 model and the Qwen2.5-72B control start from the same Qwen-written prefix; 16 completions per condition at temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95, paired by problem. The length sweep uses 0.5B, 1.5B and 7B with L in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64} from the reference midpoint; full-suffix handovers add 32B and vary the start fraction over {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9}.

Shown for Qwen2.5 models completing Qwen2.5-72B prefixes on GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval, with prefixes cut at character fractions snapped to word boundaries.

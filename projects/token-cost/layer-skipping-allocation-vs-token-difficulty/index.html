Layer-Skipping Allocations vs Cross-Model Token Difficulty

Do layer-skipping models spend more depth on the tokens that need larger models?

Answer

There is a weak positive association overall. Du et al. (2026) compared FlexiDepth's learned layer allocations with their reproduction indices, which record the smallest model in a family's size ladder that reproduces each token given the correct prefix. On 600 released generations, the pooled partial rank correlations were 0.209 using the Qwen panel and 0.213 using the OLMo panel.

The data are the released FlexiPatterns records: 600 generations and 82,783 labelled native tokens from FlexiDepth, a learned layer-skipping policy within Llama-3-8B. Allocations and panel indices are aligned to words, the first word of each response is dropped because it includes prefill effects, each task split's mean is subtracted, and the partial rank correlation controls for log position and word length.

The association varies widely by task (Table A7, 100 responses per split). Copy shows almost none, 0.061 and 0.062 over 24,770 words, and repeat is low at 0.130 and 0.120. Continue (0.277 and 0.267), summarize (0.283 and 0.294) and add (0.289 and 0.291) are moderate, and product is highest at 0.405 and 0.389. On the 68 verified addition outputs, the Qwen-panel correlation is 0.273. The multiplication split compares allocations on incorrect generations, with no verified subset.

The two quantities measure different things: the reproduction index asks which complete model can reproduce a token, while learned depth is an internal allocation within one model. The paper places this comparison alongside other within-model allocation methods such as CALM and Mixture-of-Depths.

Setup

Released FlexiPatterns records of FlexiDepth (learned layer skipping in Llama-3-8B): 600 generations, 82,783 labelled native tokens across six task splits (copy, continue, summarize, repeat, add, product). Reproduction indices come from the Qwen2.5 and OLMo-2 panels; partial rank correlations control log position and word length, with bootstrap SDs.

Shown on FlexiDepth's released task splits only; most splits are not checker-verified.

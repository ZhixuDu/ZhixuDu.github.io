How Many Large-Model Tokens a 0.5B Model Can Reproduce

What fraction of a large model's output tokens can a 0.5B model predict correctly under teacher forcing?

Answer

Most of them. Du et al. (2026) find that Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, given the prompt and the correct preceding tokens, reproduces 91.86-95.24% of the tokens in verified Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct responses on GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval (shown as 91.9%, 94.2% and 95.2% in Figure 1). The smallest members of other families behave similarly on the same references: OLMo-2 1B reproduces 89.34-91.74% and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5B reproduces 89.80-92.27%.

The measurement is teacher-forced. Each model receives the prompt rendered with its own chat template, the reference answer is appended, and vLLM returns log probabilities for every answer position. A token counts as reproduced if it is the model's top-ranked prediction. Each model always receives the reference prefix, even where its own previous prediction disagreed, and every model is evaluated at every position. The same 1,800 Qwen-written references (1,281 GSM8K, 375 MATH-500 and 144 HumanEval) are scored by all three panels in their native tokenizations.

The remaining tokens carry much of the compute. In the Qwen panel the most expensive 10% of tokens account for 63.66-79.54% of estimated FLOPs, and positions that no panel model reproduces are rare: 0.76%, 0.49% and 0.14% for the three datasets.

High agreement under the correct prefix does not mean the small model can generate unassisted. When the 0.5B model generates 64 tokens from the verified midpoint before Qwen2.5-72B resumes, accuracy falls by 9.44 points relative to an uninterrupted 72B continuation, and completing the whole response from the prompt costs 54.08 points. Exact matching also ignores alternative wordings that would be equally valid.

Setup

Measurement panels of fifteen models: Qwen2.5 (0.5B to 72B, seven sizes), OLMo-2 (1B, 7B, 13B, 32B) and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen (1.5B, 7B, 14B, 32B), with Falcon3 (1B, 3B, 7B, 10B) for cross-family replication. References are checker-verified responses from Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct (four samples per prompt at temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95, earliest passing sample kept), with Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct as a second generator. Core datasets are GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval (1,800 Qwen-written references: 1,281, 375 and 144), within twelve benchmarks totaling 50,019 prompts. Teacher-forced scoring runs in vLLM 0.14.1 with BF16 on eight 80 GB A100-SXM4 GPUs; uncertainty uses 2,000 item-bootstrap resamples.

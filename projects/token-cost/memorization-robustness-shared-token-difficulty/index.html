Memorization Checks on Cross-Model Agreement on Hard Tokens

Is cross-model agreement on hard tokens explained by memorization or benchmark contamination?

Answer

The checks in Du et al. (2026) suggest memorization does not drive it, though they cannot rule out training overlap. A screening campaign tested answer recall without demonstrated solution ability and continuation of problem text across six model sizes (Qwen 0.5B, 1.5B, 7B and 72B, OLMo 7B and 32B). It flagged 33 of the 1,758 intersection items. Removing them left the Qwen/OLMo partial correlations at 0.321 and 0.416 on Qwen- and Llama-written text, against 0.322 and 0.417 originally. On 5,000 transformed GSM-Symbolic prompts the correlations were 0.319 and 0.384.

Other controls move the estimate by less than 0.05. Adding word length and token counts gives 0.321/0.416; adding frequency and approximate entropy 0.299/0.406; adding both families' loss gaps 0.276/0.409; adding smallest-model surprisals 0.322/0.409. Restricting to high-confidence reference words gives 0.287/0.400. Changing the success criterion to top-five gives 0.302/0.443 and to p of at least 0.5 gives 0.355/0.451. Scoring a second verified response per problem gives 0.328/0.409.

The association is positive on all twelve benchmarks. On Qwen-written text, per-dataset values range from 0.286 on MBPP+ to 0.440 on HumanEval, and on Llama-written text NQ-Open reaches 0.555. Splitting MATH-500 by difficulty gives 0.315/0.460 for levels 1 to 3 and 0.337/0.464 for levels 4 and 5. On text from the Qwen3-30B-A3B mixture-of-experts generator the pooled correlation is 0.520 across 293,082 words.

The paper states that screening does not certify that unflagged items were absent from training, that GSM-Symbolic's public release does not establish absence either, and that independently developed families may share training data.

Setup

Measurement panels of fifteen models: Qwen2.5 (0.5B to 72B, seven sizes), OLMo-2 (1B, 7B, 13B, 32B) and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen (1.5B, 7B, 14B, 32B), with Falcon3 (1B, 3B, 7B, 10B) for cross-family replication. References are checker-verified responses from Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct (four samples per prompt at temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95, earliest passing sample kept), with Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct as a second generator. Core datasets are GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval (1,800 Qwen-written references: 1,281, 375 and 144), within twelve benchmarks totaling 50,019 prompts. Teacher-forced scoring runs in vLLM 0.14.1 with BF16 on eight 80 GB A100-SXM4 GPUs; uncertainty uses 2,000 item-bootstrap resamples. Extended benchmarks: LiveCodeBench subset, MBPP+, AIME 2024, ARC-Challenge, BBH, MMLU, MMLU-Pro, HellaSwag and NQ-Open, plus GSM-Symbolic.

Sensitivity analyses on the evaluated panels and benchmarks; they test particular alternatives and do not prove absence of memorization.

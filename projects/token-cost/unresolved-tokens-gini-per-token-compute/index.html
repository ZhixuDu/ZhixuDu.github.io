Unresolved Tokens and Gini Inequality of Per-Token Compute

How sensitive are token compute concentration measurements to tokens no model in the panel can reproduce?

Answer

The concentration result weakens but holds when unresolved tokens are dropped. Du et al. (2026) report that under the plotted convention, which prices each unresolved token at twice the FLOPs of the panel's largest model, the top 10% of tokens hold 63.66-79.54% of estimated FLOPs in the Qwen panel. Among resolved tokens only, the top 10% hold 51-60% for Qwen2.5, 40-44% for OLMo-2 and 26-32% for DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen, and Gini coefficients of resolved costs range from 0.218 to 0.547.

The convention matters because an unresolved position has no observed successful agent and so no finite sufficient compute under the paper's definition. The factor of two is an assumption for visualisation; it does not say what an untested model would cost or that the token truly needs a larger model. Unresolved rates on Qwen-written references are 0.76%, 0.49% and 0.14% for the Qwen panel on GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval; 2.80%, 2.99% and 2.08% for OLMo-2; and 5.73%, 4.77% and 3.59% for R1-Distill-Qwen. On Llama-written references the OLMo panel reaches 8.72% on MATH-500.

The resolved-only Gini coefficients (Table A5) use measured costs without imputation. On Qwen-written text they are 0.547, 0.451 and 0.514 for the Qwen panel and 0.330, 0.365 and 0.316 for OLMo-2. On Llama-written text they are 0.543, 0.547 and 0.243 for Qwen and 0.385, 0.467 and 0.218 for OLMo-2, in the order GSM8K, MATH-500, HumanEval. Pooled over the Qwen panel's resolved tokens, 3.03% of tokens carry half the assigned FLOPs.

The two conventions summarise different populations, since the resolved-only calculation uses resolved tokens for both the percentile and the total. Allocation is uneven under both.

Setup

Measurement panels of fifteen models: Qwen2.5 (0.5B to 72B, seven sizes), OLMo-2 (1B, 7B, 13B, 32B) and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen (1.5B, 7B, 14B, 32B), with Falcon3 (1B, 3B, 7B, 10B) for cross-family replication. References are checker-verified responses from Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct (four samples per prompt at temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95, earliest passing sample kept), with Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct as a second generator. Core datasets are GSM8K, MATH-500 and HumanEval (1,800 Qwen-written references: 1,281, 375 and 144), within twelve benchmarks totaling 50,019 prompts. Teacher-forced scoring runs in vLLM 0.14.1 with BF16 on eight 80 GB A100-SXM4 GPUs; uncertainty uses 2,000 item-bootstrap resamples.

Reported for the three core datasets on Qwen- and Llama-written verified references, using architecture-derived logical FLOPs.

Where MoE-Mamba, BlackMamba, Jamba and Routing Mamba Put Experts

How do MoE-Mamba, BlackMamba, Jamba and Routing Mamba differ in where they apply experts?

Answer

They differ mainly in whether experts sit outside or inside the SSM token mixer. According to the related work of Swimba (Du et al., 2026), the prevailing strategy is block-mixing: standard SSM layers interleaved with MoE MLP layers. BlackMamba and MoE-Mamba do this by replacing Mamba's internal MLP or interleaving MoE blocks. Jamba and Zamba combine Mamba, attention and MoE MLP layers into hybrid macro-architectures. The NVIDIA Nemotron-H family and Nemotron-3 series interleave Mamba-2 token mixers with MoE feed-forward networks at production scale. In all of these, the SSM dynamics remain dense and sparsity is extrinsic to the state transition.

Routing Mamba (RoM) moves toward inner mixing by applying MoE to the linear projections surrounding the SSM core. The Swimba paper argues that this line of work is primarily engineering-motivated and does not distinguish between two MoE-SSM designs: MoE over separated SSMs, where each expert keeps its own state trajectory and compute grows with experts, and MoE-parameterized SSMs, where expert parameters are mixed and one recurrence is run.

Swimba takes the parameter-mixed approach explicitly. It routes experts to generate the B_t and C_t (and injected input) that govern the state update, shares the transition A across experts, and aggregates parameters rather than outputs, so it keeps a single coherent state trajectory evaluated once with Mamba-2's SSD kernel. The paper supports this with theorems on single-SSM structure, recurrence cost independent of expert count, stability, the relation to separated experts, and expressivity.

Empirically, Swimba-14B adds 4 experts with top-1 routing to every Mamba-2 layer of Nemotron-H-8B and raises average benchmark accuracy from 69.47 to 70.81 at FLOPs per token within 0.2%. The paper does not run experimental comparisons against BlackMamba, MoE-Mamba, Jamba or Routing Mamba.

Setup

Backbone: Nemotron-H-8B, a 52-layer hybrid decoder with four self-attention layers and alternating Mamba-2 and FFN layers. Swimba-14B replaces every Mamba-2 layer with a Swimba layer of 4 experts, top-1 routing per token. New expert and router parameters are trained with supervised fine-tuning on the SFT portion of the Llama-Nemotron Post-Training Dataset while the copied backbone is frozen. Accuracy is measured with LM-Evaluation-Harness on ARC-Challenge, ARC-Easy, BoolQ, HellaSwag, MMLU, OpenBookQA, PIQA, RTE and WinoGrande; FLOPs with the PyTorch profiler; throughput and latency with vLLM (Transformers backend) on a single 40GB A100 GPU. Baseline: Nemotron-H-8B.

The comparison with other MoE-SSM models is qualitative; the only experimental baseline is Nemotron-H-8B.

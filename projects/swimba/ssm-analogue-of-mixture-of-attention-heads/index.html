An SSM Analogue of Mixture-of-Attention-Heads and SwitchHead

Is there an SSM analogue of mixture-of-attention-heads or SwitchHead?

Answer

Swimba (Du et al., 2026) is presented as such an analogue. Most MoE work applies experts to feed-forward blocks, and the paper cites SwitchHead and MoA (Mixture-of-Attention-Heads) as showing the utility of routing within token-mixing layers. Swimba adapts this idea to selective state space models: in each Mamba-2 layer, the in-projection is replaced by several expert projections and a token-level router.

The motivation is the compute profile of token mixers. Attention scales as O(L^2) in sequence length while SSMs scale as O(L). The paper's Figure 1 illustrates that MoE can increase parameter count with only a small increase in per-token compute, so MoE inside an SSM mixer can retain O(L) complexity while increasing layer capacity. The obstacle is specific to recurrences: the state update is the dominant cost, and a naive design that gives each expert its own recurrence scales compute with the number of experts. Attention-based MoE does not face this particular issue in the same form.

Swimba avoids it by mixing expert-produced injection and readout streams in parameter space, sharing the transition, and running one recurrence. Theorems in the paper show the result is a single selective SSM with state size independent of the number of experts and with recurrence cost that does not scale with experts.

In practice, Swimba-14B with 4 experts and top-1 routing has FLOPs per token within 0.2% of Nemotron-H-8B and an average benchmark accuracy of 70.81 versus 69.47, with as low as a 10% slowdown in vLLM decoding.

Setup

Backbone: Nemotron-H-8B, a 52-layer hybrid decoder with four self-attention layers and alternating Mamba-2 and FFN layers. Swimba-14B replaces every Mamba-2 layer with a Swimba layer of 4 experts, top-1 routing per token. New expert and router parameters are trained with supervised fine-tuning on the SFT portion of the Llama-Nemotron Post-Training Dataset while the copied backbone is frozen. Accuracy is measured with LM-Evaluation-Harness on ARC-Challenge, ARC-Easy, BoolQ, HellaSwag, MMLU, OpenBookQA, PIQA, RTE and WinoGrande; FLOPs with the PyTorch profiler; throughput and latency with vLLM (Transformers backend) on a single 40GB A100 GPU. Baseline: Nemotron-H-8B.

The connection to attention-head MoE is conceptual; the paper does not compare against MoE-attention models experimentally.

BIBO Stability of State Space Models With Expert Routing

Is a state space model with input-dependent expert routing BIBO stable?

Answer

Yes, under conditions on the shared transition and the mixed streams. In Swimba (Du et al., 2026), Theorem 3 states that if the transition satisfies ||A|| ≤ ρ < 1 in an induced matrix norm, and the mixed injection U_t = Σ π_{t,e} B^(e)_t X^(e)_t and mixed readout C_t = Σ π_{t,e} C^(e)_t are bounded for all t by constants U and C, then for any initial state h_0 the state satisfies ||h_t|| ≤ ρ^t ||h_0|| + (1 - ρ^t)/(1 - ρ) · U, and the output satisfies ||Y_t|| ≤ C times the same bound.

The proof unrolls the recursion, h_t = A^t h_0 + Σ A^(t-i) U_i, and applies submultiplicativity of the norm and the geometric series. The bound reflects standard SSM behavior: a stable transition forgets the distant past at a geometric rate. Token-wise expert switching can affect the state only through the injected term U_t, so bounding that term is sufficient to rule out state blow-up regardless of how often routing changes between experts.

This holds because of a design choice. In Swimba the transition A is shared across experts and time, and expert diversity enters only through the injection and readout streams B, C and X. With an expert-specific or routed transition, stability would depend on products of different transition matrices, which this theorem does not cover. The paper frames the concern as whether rapid expert switching can destabilize the dynamics, and the answer under these assumptions is that it cannot.

The theorem gives a sufficient condition, not a training procedure; the paper does not report empirical measurements of state norms in the trained Swimba-14B model.

Setup

Theoretical analysis of a selective SSM h_t = A h_{t-1} + B_t X_t, Y_t = C_t^T h_t with state size N, P channels, E experts, softmax routing probabilities and a dense or top-k active set; proofs are in Appendix A of the paper. The architecture is instantiated on Mamba-2 and evaluated as Swimba-14B (4 experts, top-1) built on Nemotron-H-8B.

The stability bound assumes a contractive transition shared across experts and bounded mixed streams; it is a theoretical guarantee without accompanying empirical stability measurements.

Source

Swimba: Switch Mamba Model Scales State Space Models. Zhixu Du, Krishna Teja Chitty-Venkata, Murali Emani, Venkatram Vishwanath, Hai (Helen) Li, Yiran Chen. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 39 (NeurIPS 2026), 2026. Evidence: Section 3.4 Theorem 3, Appendix A.3.

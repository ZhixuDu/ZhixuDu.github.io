Switching State-Space Models and Mixture-of-Experts in Mamba

How do switching state space models relate to mixture-of-experts in modern Mamba architectures?

Answer

Swimba (Du et al., 2026) relates classical switching state-space models (Ghahramani and Hinton) to one of two ways of adding experts to a selective SSM. In MoE over separated SSMs, each expert maintains its own state trajectory, h^(e)_t = A h^(e)_{t-1} + B^(e)_t X^(e)_t, and outputs are combined with routing weights. The paper notes that this resembles switching dynamics in state-space models: each expert can store its own memory, but compute and state storage grow with the number of experts.

The alternative, which Swimba adopts for Mamba-2, is an MoE-parameterized SSM: expert-produced injection and readout streams are mixed with the routing weights first, and a single shared state trajectory is evolved once. Its recurrence cost is O(T C_step + T C_mix) versus O(T E C_step) for separated experts.

Theorem 4 makes the trade-off precise. With a shared transition and zero initial states, the two designs are identical when routing weights are constant over time and readout is shared. When routing varies, the separated model can preserve expert-specific information in its own hidden states, while the mixed model aggregates those influences into one memory; the output gap is bounded by a stable recursion driven by how far each expert's injection deviates from the mixture.

The paper does not train a separated-SSM model, so the memory benefit of expert-specific trajectories is characterized only analytically. The trained Swimba-14B (4 experts, top-1) matches its baseline's FLOPs within 0.2%.

Setup

Theoretical analysis of a selective SSM h_t = A h_{t-1} + B_t X_t, Y_t = C_t^T h_t with state size N, P channels, E experts, softmax routing probabilities and a dense or top-k active set; proofs are in Appendix A of the paper. The architecture is instantiated on Mamba-2 and evaluated as Swimba-14B (4 experts, top-1) built on Nemotron-H-8B.

The relation to switching state-space models is conceptual and analytical; no switching or separated-expert SSM is trained in the paper.

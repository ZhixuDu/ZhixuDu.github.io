Scaling Mamba Parameters With Experts Without Extra Scans

Can you scale parameters of a Mamba layer with experts without running multiple scans?

Answer

Yes, if the experts are mixed before the recurrence instead of after it. In Swimba (Du et al., 2026), Theorem 2 states that the MoE-parameterized SSM layer costs O(T C_step(N, P) + T C_mix(k, P)), where T is sequence length, C_step is the cost of one state update with state size N and P channels, and C_mix is the cost of mixing at most k active expert streams per step. A separated MoE that advances all E expert states each step costs O(T E C_step(N, P)). Adding experts therefore adds parameters but does not multiply the expensive part of the computation.

The proof is direct. The parameter-mixed layer forms mixed injection and readout terms, Σ π_{t,e} B^(e)_t X^(e)_t and Σ π_{t,e} C^(e)_t, and then advances a single state trajectory for T steps. The separated design advances E trajectories per step. The extra work in the mixed design is confined to routing, the expert projections and the mixing sums. Theorem 1 complements this by showing the mixed layer is exactly one selective SSM with state size N, so Mamba-2's SSD kernel is reused unchanged.

The Swimba implementation places the experts in the Mamba-2 in-projection and uses top-1 routing, so each token runs one expert projection. In Swimba-14B (4 experts per layer, built on Nemotron-H-8B), the PyTorch profiler measures 1.51282 × 10^10 FLOPs per token versus 1.51263 × 10^10 for the baseline, a difference under 0.2%.

Wall-clock cost is a separate question. In vLLM decoding on a 40GB A100, Swimba-14B is slightly slower than Nemotron-H-8B, with as low as a 10% slowdown, which the authors attribute to routing overhead rather than to the recurrence.

Setup

Theoretical analysis of a selective SSM h_t = A h_{t-1} + B_t X_t, Y_t = C_t^T h_t with state size N, P channels, E experts, softmax routing probabilities and a dense or top-k active set; proofs are in Appendix A of the paper. The architecture is instantiated on Mamba-2 and evaluated as Swimba-14B (4 experts, top-1) built on Nemotron-H-8B.

The complexity result is asymptotic in T and E; the empirical FLOPs check covers one configuration with 4 experts and top-1 routing.

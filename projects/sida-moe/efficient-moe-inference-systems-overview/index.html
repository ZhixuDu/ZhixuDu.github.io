Systems for Efficient MoE Inference and Expert Offloading

Survey of systems for efficient mixture-of-experts inference and expert offloading

Answer

The related work of SiDA-MoE (Du et al., MLSys 2024) groups efficient MoE inference systems by what they optimize. Communication and parallelism systems include DeepSpeed-MoE, which optimizes grouping and scheduling and, in a later version, inference kernels and coordination of communication and parallelism; Tutel, which enables adaptive parallelism and pipelining at runtime; and SE-MoE, which uses communication scheduling and dynamic scheduling to overlap CPU-to-GPU movement with GPU computation. The paper characterizes these as focused on device-to-device communication without using the incoming data to decide what to keep on the GPU.

A second group enforces sparsity by task. M3ViT uses MoE for efficient on-device multi-task learning, and Edge-MoE implements M3ViT in hardware. There, the sparse expert path is imposed by the task, whereas SiDA-MoE exploits the sparsity that MoE routing already exhibits for each input. A third group, concurrent with SiDA-MoE, designs scheduling for caching experts on resource-constrained devices, including work on mitigating MoE deployment inefficiencies, EdgeMoE and dynamic expert swapping. The paper states that SiDA-MoE is orthogonal to these and that data-aware prediction and caching policies can be combined.

SiDA-MoE's own approach is data-aware look-ahead: an offline-trained predictor decides each token's expert at every layer before inference, so only those experts are loaded and expert selection runs in a parallel thread. It is also described as orthogonal to general model compression such as quantization and pruning, which can be applied to the activated experts, and to alternative routers like Hash layers and BASE layers, which can be plugged in as the predictor.

In the paper's own comparison, SiDA-MoE reports up to 3.93x throughput over the average of Standard, DeepSpeed and Tutel, and up to 80% GPU memory saving on Switch Transformers.

Setup

Empirical comparisons in the paper cover Standard, DeepSpeed inference and Tutel on Switch-base-8/64/128/256 with SST2, MRPC and MultiRC on an A100 80GB GPU; other systems are discussed qualitatively.

The positioning is the paper's related-work discussion as of 2023-2024; only DeepSpeed and Tutel were compared experimentally.

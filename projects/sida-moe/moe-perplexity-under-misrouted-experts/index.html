Perplexity and Accuracy Cost of Misrouted MoE Experts

How much does perplexity degrade when MoE experts are misrouted?

Answer

It depends on model size, and the cost can be substantial for language modeling. In SiDA-MoE (Du et al., MLSys 2024), replacing the routers of pretrained Switch Transformers with a learned predictor raised C4 perplexity from 6.68 to 18.49 for Switch-base-8, 4.93 to 11.84 for Switch-base-64, 4.86 to 11.73 for Switch-base-128, and 4.59 to 8.11 for Switch-base-256.

The paper reads this as larger MoE models having stronger resistance to expert misclassification caused by the predictor: the absolute perplexity increase shrinks from Switch-base-8 to Switch-base-256. On fine-tuned classification tasks the pattern is different. Fidelity, defined as the share of fine-tuned performance preserved, is 98.25% (SST2), 97.49% (MRPC) and 98.95% (MultiRC) for Switch-base-8, but 93.02%, 92.59% and 92.56% for Switch-base-128. In absolute terms Switch-base-128 goes from 93.57 to 87.04 on SST2 accuracy, 89.66 to 83.01 on MRPC F1 and 59.95 to 55.49 on MultiRC F1, while Switch-base-8 goes from 92.20 to 90.59, 89.14 to 86.91 and 56.70 to 56.11.

The errors come from two sources: predicting the wrong expert, and approximating the softmax scaling factor. The predictor's top-3 hit rate is 99.00% and 98.78% on SST2 for the two models but 90.49% to 91.74% on long MultiRC inputs.

These numbers are the accuracy side of a trade-off. The same predicted routing enables up to 80% GPU memory saving and up to 3.93x throughput on large models, because only predicted experts need to be resident and expert selection runs off the critical path.

Setup

Perplexity on C4 for pretrained Switch-base-8/64/128/256; downstream fidelity for Switch-base-8 and Switch-base-128 fine-tuned on SST2 (accuracy), MRPC and MultiRC (F1). The predictor is a 2-layer LSTM with SparseMax attention trained with truncated KD; top-1 predicted experts on SST2, top-3 on MRPC and MultiRC.

The degradation was measured for routing predicted by SiDA-MoE's LSTM predictor on Switch Transformers, not for random or adversarial misrouting.

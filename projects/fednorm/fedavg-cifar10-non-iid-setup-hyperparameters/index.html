FedAvg on Non-IID CIFAR-10: 2 Classes per Client Setup

What hyperparameters are used for FedAvg on CIFAR-10 with 2 classes per client?

Answer

A concrete configuration is given in Rethinking Normalization Methods in Federated Learning (Du et al., 2022): CIFAR-10 distributed across 100 devices, each holding 2 random classes with 100 samples per class, following the non-IID construction of McMahan et al. (2017). In each communication round 10 randomly sampled devices participate. Local training uses 10 local epochs, batch size 64 and SGD with learning rate 0.01, and training runs for 5000 communication rounds by default. Each experiment is repeated with three random seeds and the standard deviation is reported.

The aggregation algorithm is FedAvg. Three architectures are used: VGG-11, ResNet-18 and a small CNN whose layers are a 5x5 convolution from 3 to 6 channels, a normalization layer, a 5x5 convolution from 6 to 16 channels, a normalization layer, a fully connected layer of 120 units, a normalization layer, a fully connected layer of 84 units and a final layer of 10 outputs. The normalization slot is filled with group, layer, instance or batch normalization, with fixed batch normalization (statistics not aggregated), or left empty. Experiments ran on a server with two Intel Xeon E5-2687W CPUs and four Nvidia TITAN RTX GPUs.

Under this setup, accuracy after 5000 rounds ranges widely with normalization choice. With layer normalization the models reach 63.52% (VGG-11), 59.70% (ResNet-18) and 52.02% (CNN); with batch normalization they reach 51.56%, 34.38% and 33.29%. ResNet-18 without normalization reaches 60.73%. Layer normalization also converges fastest on all three models.

This label-skew partition is severe: each device sees only two of the ten classes, which is the setting the paper uses to study external covariate shift, the divergence of per-channel feature statistics across devices during local training.

Setup

FedAvg on CIFAR-10 with a pathological non-IID split following McMahan et al. (2017): 100 devices, each holding 2 random classes with 100 samples per class, and 10 randomly sampled devices per communication round. Local epochs E = 10, batch size 64, SGD with learning rate 0.01, 5000 communication rounds, three random seeds. Architectures: VGG-11, ResNet-18 and a small CNN (two 5x5 conv layers and three fully connected layers). Compared normalization: group, layer, instance and batch normalization, fixed batch normalization (training statistics not aggregated), and no normalization. Hardware: a server with two Intel Xeon E5-2687W CPUs and four Nvidia TITAN RTX GPUs.

This is the single data configuration used in the paper's experiments (CIFAR-10, 2 classes per device); other datasets or partitions are not reported.

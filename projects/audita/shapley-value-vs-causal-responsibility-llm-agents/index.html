Shapley Values vs Causal Responsibility for Blaming LLM Agents

Is Shapley value attribution a good way to assign blame among LLM agents?

Answer

It is a strong baseline but blind to duty. In AUDITA (Du and Chen, 2026), Shapley-value attribution was the strongest baseline on live GSM8K multi-agent pipelines, with responsibility error 0.223, against 0.589 for the Who&When LLM judge and 0.408 for single-site counterfactual scoring. The graded duty-aware verdict reached 0.170 with the answer key and 0.159 without it.

On the CulpaBench structures, Shapley averaged 0.452. It is graded, so it can represent shared causes in principle, but it has no notion of duty and spreads responsibility mass across non-culpable candidates. Per structure it erred 0.833 on redundancy, 0.667 on the inert twin, 0.095 on preemption, 0.667 on omission and 0.000 on the delegation chain, while the certified verdict scored 0.000 on all five.

The live comparison depends on model scale. On Qwen2.5-7B, Shapley was the more accurate of the two (0.041 versus 0.111). The graded verdict overtook it on Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-72B, with the margin moving from -0.070 to +0.037 to +0.053 as model capability rises. Against the LLM judge, the graded verdict kept a three- to fivefold gap on both Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-8B. The Llama evaluation was extended from 13 to 25 scoreable incidents under unchanged arms and metrics.

The paper notes that Shapley is the only one of the four baselines that carries no duty verdict, no certificate and no resistance to record forgery, and cites prior work showing that game-theoretic blame attributions, including Shapley, can misalign incentives in cooperative sequential decision making.

Setup

Live corpus: a GSM8K multi-agent pipeline with a planner, three parallel solvers, an aggregator that carries a declared normative duty, and a verifier; three independently generated corpora of 480 cases each (one development, two holdouts), with fault injection calibrated to an empirical multi-agent failure taxonomy and every label replay-verified. Models are open-weight (Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct primary, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct for additional study), served with vLLM on NVIDIA A100-80GB GPUs with greedy decoding and three generation seeds. Baselines: the Who&When LLM judge, CAR / CausalFlow single-site counterfactual scoring, and Shapley-value attribution. Metric: responsibility error, the l1 distance between reported and true graded responsibility profiles.

Shown on GSM8K pipelines across three open-weight models and on simulated CulpaBench structures, scored against duty-aware graded ground truth.

Deterministic LLM Replay for Counterfactual Agent Analysis

How to make LLM inference deterministic enough for counterfactual replay of agent runs?

Answer

Pin every component and serialise the replays. AUDITA (Du and Chen, 2026) obtains bitwise-exact replay with open-weight models served in vLLM with pinned weights, greedy decoding at temperature 0, fixed seeds, versioned tools, a seeded simulator, and a serial replay protocol under which replays run one at a time on an otherwise idle server. Serial execution is part of the pinned definition, not an optimisation: concurrent batching perturbs floating-point reduction order and breaks bitwise exactness even in batch-invariant serving modes.

Replay is used to certify causes. For each proposed cause, the engine intervenes on the cause set while holding the witness set at its recorded values, re-runs the incident slice, and checks that the risk of the adverse outcome falls by at least a declared margin. The factual replay must first reproduce the recorded trajectory exactly; a mismatch aborts certification and is itself reported as an integrity finding. A cause that fails replay is not reported (Proposition 1), so certified false positives are excluded on a pinned stack.

For hosted or non-deterministic models, the check becomes statistical. The certifier runs repeated factual and interventional replays and reports a cause only if the empirical risk drop exceeds the margin by twice a Hoeffding deviation term computed from ln(4/alpha) and the number of replays. A union bound then limits the probability of reporting a false cause to alpha per reported cause. An action-match score, the fraction of aligned actuation events that agree in type, target and parameters, is reported alongside to flag when the replayed system has drifted from the recorded one.

The architecture splits proposal from certification: one engine enumerates minimal cause-witness pairs on a structural abstraction, the other re-executes them, and a failed proposal triggers abstraction refinement rather than a report.

Setup

Open-weight models (Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct primary, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) served under vLLM with tensor-parallel serving on NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB GPUs, greedy decoding, fixed seeds and three generation seeds per experiment; the facility simulator is CPU-deterministic. Replay certifies causes on live GSM8K pipelines and CulpaBench incidents.

Bitwise exactness is claimed for pinned open-weight stacks under the serial replay protocol; hosted stacks receive a probabilistic guarantee only.

Responsibility Attribution Benchmark with Planted Ground Truth

Is there a benchmark for causal responsibility attribution with planted ground truth?

Answer

Yes. AUDITA (Du and Chen, 2026) releases CulpaBench, a benchmark of over 1,000 incidents in a deterministic multi-robot facility simulator, each with planted and analytically derived graded ground truth. Its incident structures are grounded in 42 public robot-accident records from OSHA and NIOSH, and its scale is roughly 10 times the 184-trace Who&When benchmark. The benchmark, generators and ground-truth specifications are released with the code.

Each structure targets a specific way single-culprit attribution fails: redundancy (two sufficient causes, 1/2 each), inert twin (a transcript-identical command whose effect never arrived), preemption, omission, delegation chain (originator versus faithful conduit), unexpected startup, record attack, a no-cause control and a trivial control. A parameterised generator also composes four monotone gadgets into random incidents; in a sampled set of 1,002 incidents, 57.8% were adverse.

Ground truth comes from exact world re-execution: the world is re-run with a candidate set of commands treated as absent, and the declared predicate, such as human-robot separation below 0.5 m, is evaluated on the result. No language model participates. The responsibility profile is computed by an analytic oracle (prime-implicant extraction followed by minimal-transversal enumeration) that shares no code with the engine under test, and is reported in a causal layer and a culpable layer.

On the five core structures, the certified verdict scored 0.000 error over 300 draws each. LLM judges averaged 0.303 to 0.343, single-site scoring 0.433, Shapley attribution 0.452 and an anomaly detector 0.900. On the 579 adverse sampled draws, the verdict had 0.000 error and exact cause-set match 1.000, against 0.370 for single-site scoring.

The paper states two provenance limits: overdetermination does not occur in the accident corpus, where every multi-failure incident is conjunctive, and delegation chains appear only once.

Setup

CulpaBench: over 1,000 incidents in a deterministic multi-robot facility simulator, with structures grounded in 42 public OSHA/NIOSH robot-accident records and ground truth computed by exact world re-execution and an analytic oracle that shares no code with the engine. Each structure is scored over 300 draws and three generation seeds. Baselines: three Who&When LLM judge formats (all-at-once, step-wise, binary-search) on Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct, CAR / CausalFlow single-site scoring, Shapley-value attribution, and a statistical anomaly detector.

Incidents are simulated in a deterministic kinematic facility and limited to discrete threshold events; structures are grounded in, not drawn from, real accident records.
